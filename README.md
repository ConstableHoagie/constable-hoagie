# 🥪 Constable Hoagie v1.4.0
### Protecting & Serving Your Dough

- Turns today's news and prices into a few simple money moves. Small bites, no homework.
- Shows everything you own, what you spend and (on the full menu) every bet, in one place.
- Never moves your money on its own. Every number is an estimate, not financial advice.

## Start in 3 steps

1. Double-click **Start Constable Hoagie**. First time, it installs what it needs (about 2 minutes).
2. Pick your look: **Original** (sunny, family-friendly) or **Dark Humor** (18+, neon, edgy jokes, no nudity).
3. Take the 2-minute tour with practice data, pick an AI brain, and connect only what you want.

**App vs website:** the website (beta.constablehoagie.com) is the same shop served from the owner's PC, so it's only up while that PC is on. Marked **App** = only on your PC; **Website** = needs the public address; **Both** = works either way.

## For admins: adding people & access

1. Open the shop on your PC (or sign in as a Zero Cool admin) and go to 🔌 Connect › 🔑 User accounts.
2. Type a username, a password (10+ characters) and pick a tier: Regular or Zero Cool. Press Add.
3. Open 🕶 The Gibson › 👥 People & links. Press 🔗 New sign-in link and send the website link privately (text or DM). It signs them straight in; making a new one kills the old one, and Revoke turns it off.
4. Same screen: change anyone's tier, or tick 🏈 Sports tab to give a money-only person a separate tab with games and the Bet Tracker.
5. On their first visit everyone gets the look picker, Hoagie's welcome, the guided tour and the AI question, once. Each person picks their own menu (money-only or full). Replays live in 👤 Me.
6. Autopatcher (once): double-click Setup Everything and say yes to GitHub. It makes the repo, installs the workflows, the Claude GitHub App and the Claude token, and switches on reports. Claude's fixes then show up there for any Zero Cool admin to Approve & release or Reject, and each admin can set their own email or Discord alerts.
7. Giving someone their own copy of the app? Leave out app\seed unless they should be a Zero Cool admin on their PC.

**Zero Cool** = god-mode admin (everything plus the 🕶 Admin tab) · **Regular** = the shop without keys, settings or admin.
